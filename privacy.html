Privacy Policy

Chess Reactor Uploader (the "App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Chess Reactor channel.

Data the App uses

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when the account owner authorizes the App.
  • Basic account information (user.info.basic) used to confirm which account is connected.
  • The operator's own video files and captions, which are uploaded to the account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are kept in the operator's private storage and are used only to call the TikTok API for the operator's own account. The App does not collect data from other TikTok users, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Removal

The account owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings; stored tokens are then deleted.
