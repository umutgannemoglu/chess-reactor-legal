Chess Reactor Uploader

Chess Reactor Uploader is a private publishing tool used by the Chess Reactor channel. It sends Chess Reactor's own chess videos to the Chess Reactor TikTok account's inbox as drafts. The creator then reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to post it.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other TikTok account.
